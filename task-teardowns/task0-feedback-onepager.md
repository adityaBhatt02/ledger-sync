# Task 0 — Referral Feedback One-Pager

Referred Simplify Money to 3 friends. Asked each to install, complete profile, use it for a few days, and share what they honestly thought.

---

## Aayushi (Working professional, Android, 1.5 yrs experience)

**Setup:** Smooth until Gmail connect. Took about 3 minutes total. Liked that it showed "what we left alone" after connecting Gmail.

**What she liked:**
- The spending pattern comparison across months ("₹957 under your usual") — said this single insight was worth the install
- Kuber.AI felt surprisingly natural in Hindi. Asked it "mera paisa kahan ja raha hai" and got a sensible answer
- Dark theme looks premium, doesn't feel like a free app

**What she didn't like:**
- "The app wants SMS, Gmail, AND my bank login. I gave SMS and Gmail but I'm not linking my bank. That's where I stopped." She never completed the full setup.
- Financial Health score says 605 but there's no scale shown. "605 out of what? Is this like CIBIL? I googled it and found nothing."
- Opened the app after 2 days and it sent zero notifications or reminders. "I forgot it existed. If it's tracking my money, tell me something useful every day."
- The "Invest your surplus" button on Runway feels pushy. "I came to track spending, not buy mutual funds."

---

## Deepak (Final year B.Tech student, Android, SBI account)

**Setup:** Had trouble because SBI SMS format wasn't fully picked up. Only 11 transactions detected out of what he estimates was 25+.

**What he liked:**
- The gamification angle — the 605 score made him want to "improve it" even though he doesn't know what it measures
- Digital gold from ₹10 is a cool hook, something different from other finance apps
- Category detection got Food & Dining right for Swiggy and Zomato

**What he didn't like:**
- "More than half my transactions are missing. If I can't trust the total, the whole app is pointless." Suspects SBI's SMS format isn't fully supported.
- The "Learn" section has modules and quizzes but they felt generic. "It's the same '50-30-20 rule' content every finance app has. Nothing specific to my situation."
- Tried to add a cash expense manually — the flow works but it's buried under a small "+" button. "I spend cash at least 5 times a day. If manual entry isn't dead simple, I won't do it."
- Insurance and gold sections feel out of place. "I downloaded a spending tracker and it's also trying to sell me insurance? Pick a lane."

---

## Aditya S. (Freelancer, multiple accounts — ICICI + Paytm Payments Bank)

**Setup:** Connected ICICI fine. Paytm Payments Bank wasn't in the bank list at all.

**What he liked:**
- The "unlink and delete everything" option in Settings gave him confidence to try it. Knows he can bail.
- Month-over-month spending chart is immediately useful without clicking into anything
- Kuber.AI understood "how much did I spend on food" and gave a real number

**What he didn't like:**
- Paytm Payments Bank not supported. "That's where half my freelance payments land. The app sees half my financial life."
- Runway page says "NEEDS ATTENTION" in yellow but everything below it is ₹0 or vague. "Attention for what? Just tell me what to do."
- The app groups all his freelance income as one blob. No way to tag clients or split by project. "For salaried people maybe this works, but freelancers need to know which client paid what."
- Credit card section detected his ICICI card but showed no statement data after 3 days. "Connected it, gave permissions, nothing happened. No error either — just empty."

---

## Summary — What They Didn't Like (common across all 3)

1. **Too many permissions before showing value.** All 3 hesitated at the SMS + Gmail + bank login combo. Show something useful after the first permission — don't gatekeep behind three.

2. **Incomplete bank support breaks trust.** If the app misses transactions because it can't parse a bank's SMS format, the totals are wrong and users know it. SBI and Paytm Payments Bank gaps were deal-breakers.

3. **Feature sprawl dilutes the core.** Spending tracker + insurance + digital gold + mutual funds + quizzes — users came for one thing (track spending) and felt the app was trying to do everything. The core tracking needs to be airtight before adding financial products.
